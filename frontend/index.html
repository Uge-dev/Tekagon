Frequently Asked Questions

About

What digital services does Tekagon provide? +

Tekagon provides brand identity and strategy, UI/UX product design, frontend web development, backend systems engineering, mobile application development, DevOps and cloud infrastructure, digital marketing, technical consulting, and technical writing. We help businesses plan, design, build, launch, and grow secure digital products that customers can actually use.

Can Tekagon build a website, mobile app, and brand identity together? +

Yes. Tekagon can handle the complete product journey, from brand strategy, logo systems, and visual identity to responsive websites, mobile apps, dashboards, backend APIs, databases, booking systems, chat features, and admin portals. This keeps your digital experience consistent from first impression to final launch.

Why choose Tekagon for full-stack web development and UI/UX design? +

Tekagon combines strategy, design, frontend engineering, backend development, and growth thinking in one workflow. That means your website or application is not only visually polished, but also fast, responsive, scalable, searchable, and structured around your business goals.

Getting Started

How do I start a project with Tekagon? +

You can start by creating an account, booking a session, or submitting a service request from the dashboard. Once your request is received, Tekagon reviews your goals, timeline, budget, and project details so the right solution can be recommended before development begins.

How does Tekagon manage communication during a project? +

Communication is handled through the Tekagon dashboard, live chat, tickets, email updates, and booked sessions when needed. This gives each project a clear record of requests, decisions, files, timelines, and support conversations.

Membership

How much does a website, mobile app, or branding project cost? +

Project cost depends on scope, features, design complexity, content readiness, integrations, and launch timeline. A simple brand or landing page costs less than a full web platform, mobile app, payment system, or custom backend. Tekagon reviews each request and provides a package that fits the work required.

Can Tekagon support or maintain an existing website or app? +

Yes. Tekagon can improve, redesign, debug, secure, optimize, or maintain existing websites, dashboards, APIs, mobile apps, and business platforms. This includes performance fixes, UI upgrades, backend updates, hosting support, analytics, SEO improvements, and feature expansion.

Does Tekagon work with startups, small businesses, and growing brands? +

Yes. Tekagon works with startups, SMEs, personal brands, creators, agencies, and established businesses that need professional digital products. Whether you need a first website or a complete technology system, the process is shaped around your current stage and growth goals.

Scope

What technologies does Tekagon use for web and mobile development? +

Tekagon uses modern design and engineering tools depending on the project, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React-style workflows, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Firebase, cloud hosting, API integrations, email services, analytics, and design tools such as Figma and Adobe Creative Suite.

What is Tekagon's website and app development process? +

The process starts with discovery and requirements, then moves into structure, UI/UX design, development, database and API setup, testing, deployment, and post-launch support. For SEO-focused websites, Tekagon also considers content structure, loading speed, responsive design, metadata, and conversion paths.

Can Tekagon help with SEO, digital growth, and business automation? +

Yes. Tekagon can support SEO-friendly website structure, landing pages, content planning, digital marketing workflows, analytics, lead capture forms, booking systems, email notifications, customer dashboards, and automation that helps your business attract, convert, and retain more customers.
